# Growth & Marketing — fixing the distribution gap

Acquired micro-SaaS almost always has **"engineer brain"**: great code, broken distribution.
These are the levers, ranked by typical ROI.

## 1. Fix the funnel — AARRR
**A**cquisition → **A**ctivation → **R**etention → **R**eferral → **R**evenue.
Most failed indie apps win Acquisition (a Product Hunt spike) and then **leak at Activation and
Retention**. Diagnose where the bucket leaks before pouring in more traffic.

- **Time-to-Value (TTV) is the #1 fix.** User must hit the "Aha!" moment within ~60 seconds of
  signup. Strip every step between signup and core value (target <3 clicks).
- Use a **PLG / hybrid funnel**: let the product sell itself (free trial / freemium that reaches
  value fast), layer sales-assist only on the high tiers.

## 2. Pricing psychology (often the fastest revenue lift)
- **Raise prices.** *The Bannerbear effect:* Jon Yongfook raised pricing $9 → $49 and churn
  **dropped** — cheap plans attract high-support, high-churn bargain hunters; serious users want a
  sustainable price.
- **Eliminate Lifetime Deals.** An inherited LTD is a cash bomb. First move is migrating to clean
  monthly/annual recurring tiers.
- **Anchor & frame.** Put the highest enterprise tier on the **left** so the standard tier looks
  cheap by comparison. Use **charm pricing** ($49 vs $50).

## 3. Value-proposition clarity
Replace vague headers with concrete utility:
- ❌ "An innovative solution for managing your digital assets."
- ✅ "Compress 4K video files by 80% in your browser without losing quality."
Lead with the outcome and the number.

## 4. Distribution channels that work now
- **LinkedIn founder profiles** in B2B SaaS often outperform corporate pages **>10×**. Run a
  "build in public" loop posting engineering fixes and micro-metric milestones.
- **FAQ / content clustering.** Modern buyers (and AI search) solve highly specific problems.
  Build structural content clusters around long-tail user questions instead of generic blog posts —
  this compounds as durable organic acquisition.

## 5. "Different, not Better" — the transformation protocol
When an asset has **strong retention but low MRR**, don't try to out-build incumbents. Differentiate:
- **Workflow over LLM.** Don't upgrade the underlying model. Build hard-coded native integrations
  (Zapier, webhooks) and specialized UI that make the output **immediately actionable**.
- **Vertical pivot.** Narrow the ICP from "everyone" to an unsexy, high-value industry (logistics,
  municipal legal compliance, franchise supply chain). A wedge beats a feature.
- **Monetization pivot.** Move from a flat $9/mo tier to **usage- or outcome-based** pricing that
  charges for value delivered (e.g. per audited invoice).

> Prioritize with effort × impact. In almost every flip the order is: **TTV/Activation → pricing
> re-anchor → retention email → content/distribution → differentiation pivot.** Capture the
> before/after of each so the resale story writes itself.
